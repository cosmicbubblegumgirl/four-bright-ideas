YOUR STUDY SPACE, WITHIN REACH

A little icon.
A whole lot of possibility.

Keep Study Zone on your iPhone or iPad Home Screen, with its own app window and your study tools a tap away.

Make room for your next breakthrough.

  1. Open Study Zone in Safari.
  2. Tap Safari’s Share button. You may need to open the menu first.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen. If it is hidden, scroll through the available actions.
  4. Keep Open as Web App enabled if that option appears, then tap Add.
Open Study Zone ↗

Your subjects. Your pace.

Find revision notes, worked examples, practice, a study planner and a little breathing room. Once you’ve opened the app online and its content has finished downloading, included study tools can work offline. Shared classes, account actions, videos, external papers and WhatsApp need a connection.

Your Home Screen study data can be separate from Safari data. Use Export / Import to move your notes and plan, and keep a backup before deleting the app.
