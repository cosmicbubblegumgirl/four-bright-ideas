# Ledger Legends

A Grade 12 Accounting study studio by Simoné Govender.

Topic notes, numerical practice variations, concept checks, mini mocks, calculators, flashcards, study planning, notes, printable worksheets and device-local progress with backup export/import.

Open `index.html` through a static web server. The shared modules are in `../study-kit/`. No build step or account is required. GitHub Pages hosts the app at:

https://cosmicbubblegumgirl.github.io/four-bright-ideas/accounting/

Practice is original revision material; official papers and memoranda are linked to DBE collections with their original credits. External video searches are clearly marked. Study records are saved in local storage on the current device. The service worker caches the app for offline study after a successful initial load; external resources require internet access.

## Past-paper bank

Selected November 2023–2025 DBE questions from both papers, with adapted summaries, original exam figures, worked explanations and links to the official paper collections and marking guidelines. Search by topic, year and question reference. Learners can check final numerical answers, self-review written responses, save questions and their own working, and print a six-question practice set with a worked guide.
