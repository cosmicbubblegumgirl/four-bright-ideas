# Quantum Jump

A Grade 12 Physical Sciences study space by Simoné Govender, Bachelor of Education and full-stack web developer.

[Open Quantum Jump](https://cosmicbubblegumgirl.github.io/four-bright-ideas/quantum-jump/)

Choose Physics or Chemistry, explore a topic, watch a lesson and practise the working. Each of the 20 topics includes at least 60 credited YouTube lessons and 100 practice questions. Official papers, memos and interactive simulations support exam preparation.

Learners can keep notes, track their progress, plan study sessions and ask Newton for help. Text, contrast, motion and focus settings can be adjusted to suit the learner.

Original learning resources carry Simoné’s credit. External lessons, papers and simulations retain their original creators’ credits.
